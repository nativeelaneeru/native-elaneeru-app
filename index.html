Helpful answers

Know before you order.

Which app should I use?

Homes and individual customers should use Direct. Approved local coconut vendors and business buyers should use Business.

Why are Business prices not public?

Business pricing can depend on the approved vendor, product, quantity and validity period. Sign in to see your assigned price.

Can I install the apps?

Yes. Open Direct or Business in Chrome and use “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.

How will subscriptions work?

Select quantity, frequency, start date, address and delivery slot, with controls to pause, skip, resume or cancel. This feature is under development.

How can a local coconut vendor join?

Submit Vendor Onboarding. Our team reviews the details before access and pricing are activated.
