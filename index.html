More than a coconut

A stronger local supply chain.

Every coconut passes through a connected journey — from sourcing and quality checks to inwarding, dispatch, roadside vendors and customers. We are designing that journey to be simpler, more visible and more dependable.

01 • SourceBuild consistent farm-side supply.
02 • MoveTrack stock through operations.
03 • ServeSupport vendors and customers.
01 — Sourcing

It starts at the source.

We coordinate fresh tender coconut supply with growers and trusted partners so quality and availability are planned before stock reaches the city.

02 — Inwarding

Every bunch enters with visibility.

Stock moves through inwarding and inventory controls, helping the team know what arrived, what is available and where it should move next.

03 — Vendor supply

Built for roadside vendors.

Approved vendors get a dedicated business experience for dependable sourcing, clear pricing and repeat ordering without mixing consumer and vendor workflows.

04 — Customer

Freshness reaches everyone.

Homes and individual customers can order through Native Elaneeru Direct while the same connected operation keeps the supply chain moving behind the scenes.
