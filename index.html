Why Native Elaneeru exists

This is bigger than selling coconuts.

Across our streets, tender-coconut vendors build their livelihood one customer at a time. They are small businesses, but they are an important part of everyday local life.

Native Elaneeru exists to stand beside that vendor. We want local sellers to spend less energy worrying about sourcing and more energy serving their customers, growing their business and earning with confidence.

At the same time, we want the value created by this ecosystem to travel back toward the farmers who grow the product at its source.

We are not trying to replace the local coconut vendor. We are building to make the local coconut vendor stronger.

Our vendor promise

Built around the vendor. Not around a template.

A Native Elaneeru vendor should feel that the company understands the street, the season, the customer and the pressure of running a small daily business.

01Dependable support

We aim to make the vendor's everyday business easier and more predictable — without publishing the internal systems behind that promise.

02Vendor-first relationship

Technology should support the vendor, not make the vendor work for the technology. Our team remains part of the relationship.

03Local business growth

Our success should be visible in stronger local vendors, better customer service and a healthier tender-coconut ecosystem.

What stays private: internal procurement, inwarding, stock, allocation, routing and operating methods are not presented on the public website.
