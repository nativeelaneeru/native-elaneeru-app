01 — Source

It starts at the farm.

We procure closer to the source to build better visibility on supply, freshness and quality.

02 — Procurement

Selected for the right market.

Stock is procured with the local vendor requirement in mind — quality, size, freshness and demand.

03 — Inwarding

Checked before it moves.

Every load is counted, inspected and recorded before becoming available for vendor supply.

04 — Stock visibility

Know what came in. Know where it goes.

Inwarded stock can be tracked and allocated more systematically instead of relying only on manual movement.

05 — Order & Dispatch

Right stock. Right vendor. Right time.

Vendor orders are prepared from available stock, checked, allocated, loaded and dispatched.

06 — Vendor

Fresh supply reaches the local seller.

Reliable replenishment helps roadside vendors maintain availability, serve more customers and build a more stable business.
