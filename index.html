Our Coconut Journey

A supply chain built around the vendor.

Instead of showing generic promises, this is how Native Elaneeru is designed to move tender coconuts from source to local seller.

01

Source from Farms

Work closer to the source to build better visibility on supply, freshness and quality.

Farm → Procurement
02

Procurement

Procure stock based on quality, size, freshness and market requirement — not simply bulk movement.

Selected for the right market
03

Inwarding & Quality

Every load is counted, inspected and recorded before it becomes available for supply.

Count → Inspect → Record
04

Stock Visibility

Build visibility on what came in, what is available and where stock is allocated.

Inventory → Tracking
05

Vendor Order

Approved local vendors can place repeat orders based on their actual requirement and availability.

Simple replenishment
06

Allocation & Sorting

Available stock is allocated to vendor orders, sorted and checked before dispatch.

Right stock → Right vendor
07

Dispatch & Delivery

Orders are loaded and moved to the local roadside vendor with better operational visibility.

Load → Move → Deliver
08

Reliable replenishment helps the local seller maintain availability and serve more customers.
