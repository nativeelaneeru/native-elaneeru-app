Our Coconut Journey

Follow one coconut from source to street.

The strongest way to understand Native Elaneeru is to see how the supply moves — from farm sourcing and procurement to inwarding, stock visibility, vendor ordering, dispatch and finally the people who sell and drink it.

NE • STOCK ID
01 — Source from Farms

It starts at the farm.

We work closer to the source to procure tender coconuts with better visibility on supply and quality.

02 — Procurement

Selected for the right market.

Coconuts are procured based on quality, size, freshness and market requirement — not simply moved as bulk stock.

03 — Inwarding & Quality Check

Every load is checked before it moves forward.

At inwarding, stock is counted, inspected and recorded before becoming available for supply.

FarmVehicleNE InwardingQuality Check
04 — Stock Visibility

Know what came in. Know where it goes.

Native Elaneeru tracks inwarded stock so inventory can be allocated more systematically instead of depending only on manual movement.

05 — Vendor Orders

Built around the local coconut vendor.

Roadside vendors need dependable stock, predictable sourcing and fewer supply disruptions. Native Elaneeru helps vendors replenish without repeatedly searching for supply.

06 — Sorting & Dispatch

Right stock. Right vendor. Right time.

Vendor orders are prepared from available stock, checked and assigned for dispatch.

07 — Delivered to the Vendor

Fresh supply reaches the local seller.

Our objective is not simply to deliver coconuts. It is to help local vendors maintain availability, serve more customers and build a more stable business.

08 — Customer

And finally, freshness reaches everyone.

The customer gets fresh tender coconut while the farmer gets market access and the vendor gets dependable supply.
