What Native Elaneeru does

A stronger supply chain behind every coconut sold.

Roadside coconut vendors depend on fresh stock every day. Native Elaneeru is designed to make that supply more dependable by connecting sourcing, inwarding, inventory visibility and vendor delivery in one operating flow.

The Native Elaneeru supply journey
01 — Source

It starts at the source.

We procure closer to the origin so supply, freshness and quality are visible before stock reaches the city.

02 — Procurement

Selected for vendor demand.

Quality, size, freshness and local requirement guide what is procured — not just bulk movement.

03 — Inwarding

Every load is checked.

Stock is counted, inspected and recorded before it becomes available for vendor supply.

04 — Stock visibility

Know what came in. Know where it goes.

Available stock can be seen and allocated more systematically instead of relying only on manual movement.

05 — Order & Dispatch

Right stock. Right vendor. Right time.

Orders move through allocation, loading and dispatch from the available inwarded stock.

06 — Vendor

Fresh supply reaches the seller.

Reliable replenishment helps roadside vendors maintain availability and serve more customers every day.
