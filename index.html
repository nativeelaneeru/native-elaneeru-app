The vendor problem

Every selling day starts with a sourcing problem.

A roadside coconut vendor should be focused on selling and serving customers — not repeatedly searching for stock, negotiating every refill and wondering what will arrive next.

01

Daily sourcing uncertainty

Availability can change quickly. Vendors may need to spend time finding the next load instead of running the stall.

02

Little stock visibility

Without a structured inwarding and allocation process, it is difficult to know what stock is available and what is already committed.

03

Replenishment starts from zero

Repeat buying should be simple. Vendors should not have to rebuild the sourcing process every time stock gets low.

Behind every vendor order

An order should trigger operations, not confusion.

Native Elaneeru connects the vendor-facing order to a structured internal flow so each order can move through availability, allocation and dispatch.

01

Order received

The vendor places the required quantity through the business channel.

02

Availability checked

The order is matched against inwarded, usable stock rather than an undefined pool.

03

Stock allocated

Required inventory is reserved for that vendor order so the next movement is clear.

04

Loading & dispatch

Allocated stock is prepared, loaded and moved into the delivery cycle.

05

Vendor replenished

The vendor receives stock and can continue selling without rebuilding the sourcing process from scratch.
